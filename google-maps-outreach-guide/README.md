### 🗺️ Area Filter Karne Ka Tarika

1. Google Maps pe search karo
2. Map pe zoom in karo specific area par (DHA, Clifton, Gulshan, etc.)
3. Ya search query mein area ka naam add karo
4. Left side mein list view mein sare businesses dikhtay hain
5. Har business pe click karo — phone number, website, reviews sab mil jata hai

## Step 3: Qualifying Leads

### 🎯 Lead Priority System

#### 🔥 Priority 1: HOT LEADS
**Criteria:**
- ❌ Website nahi hai
- ⭐ Google rating 4.0 ya usse zyada
- 📍 Active business (recently reviewed)
- 📸 Acchi photos hain listing mein
- 💬 Reviews ka jawab dete hain

**Action:** Aaj hi contact karo!

---

#### 🟡 Priority 2: WARM LEADS
**Criteria:**
- 🌐 Website hai lekin purani ya mobile-friendly nahi
- ⭐ Rating 3.5 - 4.0
- 🐌 Website slowly load hoti hai
- 📱 Mobile pe properly nahi dikhti

**Action:** Website issues point out karke contact karo

---

#### 🔵 Priority 3: COLD LEADS
**Criteria:**
- ✅ Website hai lekin old/not optimized
- ⭐ Rating achi hai
- Website redesign ya additional features offer karo

**Action:** Improvement angle se contact karo

---

#### 🚫 Red Flags — Avoid Karo!
- ❌ Permanently closed businesses
- ⭐ Rating 2.5 se kam (customers khush nahi, payment issues ho sakti hai)
- 🏢 Chain/Franchise (corporate process se hoga, direct nahi)
- 📞 Phone number nahi hai listing mein
- 📅 Reviews bahut purani hain (2+ years ago)

## Step 5: Follow-up Strategy

### 📅 Follow-up Timeline

```
Day 1  ──→ First WhatsApp Message
Day 3  ──→ Soft Reminder (if no reply)
Day 7  ──→ Call (if still no reply)
Day 14 ──→ Final Follow-up + Special Offer
Day 30 ──→ Archive (mark as cold, revisit later)
```

### 📱 Follow-up Message Templates

#### Day 3 — Soft Reminder
```
[Name] bhai/sis, assalam o alaikum!

Mujhe yad aya maine aapko 3 din pehle message kiya tha web development ke 
baare mein.

Bas ek quick check — kya aap interested hain ya abhi sahi waqt nahi?

No pressure! Bas bata dein to aapka time waste nahi karta. 🙂
```

#### Day 7 — Phone Call Script
```
"Assalam o Alaikum, [Name] bhai/sahab se baat ho sakti hai?
Main [Your Name] hun — aapko web development ke baare mein message kiya tha.
Bas ek minute ka waqt chahiye tha — kya aap thodi der baat kar sakte hain?"
```

#### Day 14 — Final Follow-up + Special Offer
```
[Name] bhai/sis, last message!

Main aaj ek special offer share karna chahta hun:

🎁 Is month ke liye:
- Free domain (1 year)
- Free hosting (3 months)
- Free Google My Business setup
- 10% discount on total package

Yeh offer sirf is hafty valid hai.

Interested hain? Bas reply karein — aage ki baat karte hain! 🚀
```

## Step 9: Pro Tips 💡

### ⏰ Best Time to Message
- **Morning: 10 AM - 12 PM** ✅ (Business hours shuru, owner available)
- **Afternoon: 3 PM - 5 PM** ✅ (After lunch slump, people check phones)
- **Avoid: 1 PM - 3 PM** ❌ (Lunch break/prayer time)
- **Avoid: After 7 PM** ❌ (Family time, intrusive)

### ✍️ Personalization is KEY
- HAMESHA business ka naam use karo
- Kuch specific mention karo (unki rating, photos, location)
- Generic copy-paste messages response nahi dete
- Unke business ke baare mein ek specific compliment do

### 🔍 Research Before Reaching Out
1. Unki Google listing properly check karo
2. Website hai to check karo:
   - Mobile pe theek dikhti hai?
   - Fast load hoti hai?
   - Contact form work karta hai?
3. Instagram/Facebook check karo — active hain?
4. Google reviews read karo — kya complain hain?

### 📸 Screenshot Strategy
- Unki Google listing ka screenshot lo
- Message mein likho: "Maine aapki listing pe notice kiya..."
- Shows you did your homework!

### 🎤 Voice Notes on WhatsApp
- Text se zyada personal lagte hain
- 30-60 second ka voice note bhejo
- Clearly bolao apna naam aur business

### 💼 Industry-Specific Portfolio
- Restaurants ke liye: Restaurant website examples
- Doctors ke liye: Medical website examples
- Boutiques ke liye: Fashion website examples
- Alag alag portfolio pages banao

### 📊 Track Everything
- Kon sa message template best kaam kaya?
- Kon si area se zyada reply aya?
- Kon sa time pe message bhejne se zyada response mila?
- Data use karo apni strategy improve karne ke liye

### 🤝 Referral Strategy
- Har client se ek referral maango
- "Kya aap kisi aur ko jaante hain jise website chahiye?"
- Referral discount do (10-15%)

## Step 10: Persona-Based Targeting on Google Maps

### 🔍 Kaise Identify Karein Kaunsa Persona Hai?

#### 🚀 Startup Sajid
**Google Maps pe Signs:**
- Recently opened (1-2 years, new reviews)
- Modern concept (fusion food, tech cafe, etc.)
- Active Instagram linked in listing
- Modern photos, good aesthetics
- Younger reviewers

**Example Searches:**
```
new cafe Karachi 2024
startup restaurant DHA
tech cafe Karachi
```

---

#### 🏪 Traditional Tariq
**Google Maps pe Signs:**
- Est. many years ago (old reviews going back 5+ years)
- Traditional business (hardware, textile, wholesale)
- Old or no photos
- Website nahi ya bahut purani
- Located in Saddar, Bolton Market, SITE, Korangi

**Example Searches:**
```
hardware store Saddar
textile wholesale Karachi
manufacturer SITE area
```

---

#### 👗 Boutique Bisma
**Google Maps pe Signs:**
- Home-based or small shop
- Fashion, jewelry, bakery, crafts category
- Instagram link in listing
- Beautiful product photos
- Female owner name in listing
- Located in DHA, Clifton, Tariq Road, Gulshan

**Example Searches:**
```
boutique DHA Karachi
home bakery Karachi
handmade jewelry Clifton
ladies boutique Tariq Road
```

---

#### 🏥 Professional (Doctor/Lawyer)
**Google Maps pe Signs:**
- "Dr." ya "Advocate" name mein
- Medical/legal office category
- Professional photos
- Reviews about expertise
- Located in DHA, Clifton, PECHS, Nazimabad

**Example Searches:**
```
specialist doctor DHA
dental clinic Clifton

chartered accountant PECHS
```
