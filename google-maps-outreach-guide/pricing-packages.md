## 🎯 Pricing Tips for Closing

### Price Objection Responses:

**"Bohat mehenga hai":**
> "Main samajhta hun. Isliye installment option available hai — PKR [X] × 3 months mein. Aur yeh ek baar ka investment hai jo saalon kaam karta hai!"

**"Sochna padega":**
> "Bilkul! Kya main aapko ek written proposal bhej sakta hun with all details? Is se decision lena easy ho jayega."

**"Pehle ek sample dekha do":**
> "Zaroor! Main aapke industry mein mere kuch kiye hue websites share karta hun — aur ek free mockup bhi bana sakta hun first page ka."

**"Koi doosra kam mein kar dega":**
> "Sahi kaha — market mein sasti options hain. Lekin quality, support, aur reliability ka farq hoga. Main guarantee deta hun [X] months support ka — aur sab kuch documented hoga."
